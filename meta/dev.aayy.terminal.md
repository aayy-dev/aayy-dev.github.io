**Terminal** is a terminal app for jailbroken iPhones, based on [NewTerm 3](https://github.com/hbang/NewTerm) by HASHBANG Productions, with fixes and a few additions.

- **Text selection:** long press, double or triple tap, drag the handles, Select All or ⌘A. Copying joins lines that wrapped on screen.
- **iSH-style toolbar:** ctrl, tab, esc and fn on the left, the arrow keys in the middle, and Select All, Paste and the keyboard key on the right.
- **fn key:** Home, End, Page Up and Page Down on the arrows, F1 to F12 on the number row, and forward delete.
- **Safe paste:** at a shell prompt a paste lands on the command line and nothing runs until you press return. Pasting several lines anywhere else asks first.
- **Keyboard:** a tap shows it, selecting text hides it until your next tap, and the keyboard key or a two-finger tap shows or hides it.
- **Fixes for NewTerm's bugs:** output no longer stops once the scrollback fills up, the font preview no longer crashes, font and theme changes apply right away, plus wide characters, colors, hardware keyboards, tabs and splits, ssh:// links, bash and pastes.

Installs alongside NewTerm. Rootless jailbreak, iOS 15 or later. Source: [github.com/aayy-dev/Terminal-iOS](https://github.com/aayy-dev/Terminal-iOS)
